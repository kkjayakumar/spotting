# Privacy Policy for Spotting Capture

**Last Updated:** July 11, 2026

This privacy policy describes how **Spotting Capture** (the "Extension") handles and protects user data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Spotting Capture only collects data when explicitly requested by the user (e.g., when taking a screenshot or recording a bug walkthrough on the active tab). 

Specifically, the Extension may capture:
* **Website Content:** Screenshots and video recordings of the active tab.
* **User Activity:** User interactions (clicks, keyboard events) and diagnostic console logs on the active tab.
* **Configuration Data:** Your self-hosted API URL and Project Public Key (saved locally in your browser's storage).

## 2. Data Transmission
All captured diagnostic logs, screenshots, and video walkthroughs are transmitted directly and securely to the user-configured Spotting API server (e.g., your self-hosted instance). The Extension does not transmit any data to third-party services.

## 3. Data Retention
The Extension itself does not retain or store any captured screenshots, video recordings, or session logs. Once submitted, data retention is managed by your self-hosted Spotting server instance.

## 4. Disclosures
* We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.
* We do not use your data for marketing, advertising, or creditworthiness evaluation.
